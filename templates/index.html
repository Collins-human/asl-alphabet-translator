ASL Alphabet Translator

Real-time American Sign Language recognition using your webcam

Hold your hand clearly in the center of the camera. Good lighting helps!

Tips for best results:
• Use natural daylight or bright indoor lighting
• Keep your hand steady and well-framed
• Avoid very fast movements
• Try different distances from the camera
